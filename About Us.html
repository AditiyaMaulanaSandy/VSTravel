Our Story

How We Started

One of the leading travel agency brands in Indonesia has managed to build an extraordinary reputation in a short time. Founded in 2020 as a small travel agency in the Kemang area, South Jakarta, the company started from the dream of two brothers who had a deep passion for tourism and customer service.

Starting from a simple office with only five employees, the company has now grown into a travel agent with four strategic branches in major cities in Indonesia. The head office remains in Jakarta, while three other branches are spread across Medan (North Sumatra), and two locations in Surabaya (East Java).

Today, VSTravel continues to innovate and improve our services, always keeping our customers' needs at the heart of everything we do. We're proud to have helped connect millions of people to their dream destinations and look forward to continuing this journey for many years to come.
