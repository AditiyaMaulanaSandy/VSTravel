Frequently Asked Questions

How can I book a tour package?

+

You can book a tour package directly through our website by clicking on the "Travel Now" button, or by contacting our customer service team via phone or email.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are secured and encrypted.

Is travel insurance included in the package?

+

Travel insurance is not automatically included in our packages. However, we highly recommend purchasing travel insurance, which we can help arrange for an additional cost.

Can I customize a tour package?

+

Yes, we offer customizable tour packages. Please contact our team with your specific requirements, and we'll create a tailored itinerary for you.
